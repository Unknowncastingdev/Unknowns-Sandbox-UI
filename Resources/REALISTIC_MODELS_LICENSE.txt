Poly Haven models (https://polyhaven.com/models): CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain).
Quaternius Ultimate Guns Pack (https://quaternius.com, via https://poly.pizza): CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain).

Converted with Models/converter (convert.js): the textures are baked into one colour per
triangle and the meshes simplified. To rebuild: run fetch_ph.js with the Poly Haven ids,
download the Quaternius GLBs, then jobs.sh (needs Node with @gltf-transform/core,
@gltf-transform/extensions, meshoptimizer, jpeg-js and pngjs).

Gadget models (also CC0 1.0, via https://poly.pizza): "Scifi Pistol" by Quaternius (Teleport Gun) and
"Scifi Gun" by MiniPoly (Build Gun).
